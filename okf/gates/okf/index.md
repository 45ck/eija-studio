# Quality Gates

* [nox -s okf_structure](okf-structure.md) - OKF v0.2 conformance and internal links of the committed wiki (no source hashing, no regeneration).
* [nox -s okf_tools](okf-tools.md) - Tests of the OKF tooling itself: hash normalisation, marker-preserving regeneration, and a negative control per check.
* [nox -s okf](okf.md) - OKF v0.2 conformance, links, code-link hashes (STALE, NOTES_STALE), coverage and generator drift.
