# Personas, stories and acceptance

Prepared **2 October 2026** for the [whole-app UX specification](WHOLE-APP-UX.md). Personas are design hypotheses distilled from the owner's direction, not interviewed user segments. Human validation remains NOT_RUN. Priorities sequence work; they do not excuse an incomplete supported IDE. P0 is required for the supported IDE release; P1 is planned factory/refactoring expansion and must not appear functional before implemented.

## Four personas and competing needs

| Persona | Job and preferred starting point | Tension the design must resolve |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain engineer / modeller** | Define language and invariants; start in S01 tree/UML and S05 rules; examine exact guards and relationships | Needs precision and direct manipulation, while large forms and notation can overwhelm the prompting user. Share one model, use contextual inspectors and progressive detail, not separate simplified semantics. |
| **AI-assisted engineer** | Describe a change, inspect an agent's proposal, continue iterating quickly; start in S02 then S03 | Wants a fast loop, but unchecked interpretations or stale source can be harmful. Keep prompt entry direct, agent state visible and source-grounded review close; do not add compulsory wizard steps or let speed confer approval. |
| **Reviewer / technical lead** | Decide what changed, what can break and what evidence justifies confidence; start in S03/S06 | Needs completeness and exact revisions, while raw dumps burden comprehension. Lead with semantic changes and known impact; retain one-step access to assumptions, source and raw observations. A green summary cannot conceal unknowns. |
| **Agent-factory operator** | Coordinate concurrent sessions and branches, dependencies, semantic conflicts and landing; planned S09 entry | Wants throughput and overview, while engineers need focused local work and owners retain decision authority. Keep orchestration optional, expose exact branch/base/merge-result identities, and show overlaps without claiming a sound conflict predictor. |

One person may switch roles within a session. Views and remembered layout can adapt; model meaning, evidence truth and authority cannot. Factory convenience must not require one new worktree per minor task or turn the core product into a dashboard of disconnected agents.

## State of evidence

**INSPECTED** means relevant development source/UI elements were seen in the integration checkout on this date; it does not mean merged, complete or usable. **PLANNED** means the whole capability has no acceptance result here; partial foundations may exist. Every story's execution status in this document is **NOT_RUN**. Replace it only with exact revision/environment, command or journey, raw observation and negative-control result in the [run record](../engineering/SELF-DOGFOOD-ACCEPTANCE.md#run-record). The older HCI snapshot covers the excursion journey, not these stories.

## What closes the design and the release

Design review closes a screen only when its linked states, navigation, terminology, interactions and failure/recovery behavior are specified and inspected at the declared sizes. Implementation acceptance additionally requires actual browser/server evidence for all applicable P0 stories and the existing SD01–SD12/UX01–UX08 bar. A unit test, still image, walkthrough-only path or acceptable aggregate score cannot offset data loss, false evidence, a missed declared critical fixture or an unusable core workflow.

Use a compact evidence ledger per story: owner, dependency, exact subject, state/viewport, expected observation, result, artifact and open finding. Mark implemented, integrated, exercised and accepted separately. Re-run affected stories after a change; run heavy gates serially. Reuse HCI instrumentation and freeze human-study tasks/oracles separately under the [V&V protocol](../research/2026-10-02-vv-protocol.md). Do not report hypothetical persona success as observed comprehension improvement.
