## Problem and user goal

* Persona: P1 agent-change reviewer (primary, ICP): reads status and change kind at a glance, daily. Also P4 (reads evidence status daily), P2 (diagram overlays, categories), P3 (reads), P5 (first impression). Personas are hypotheses.
* Job to be done: when I review an AI-proposed change, I want each item's evidence status, each operation's change kind and each affected model told apart at a glance, by anyone (including me under a colour-vision deficiency or in a dark room), so I can see UNKNOWN and decide defensibly.
* Task ids and frequency: T02, T03, T06, T08, T14 (daily or weekly); T04, T07, T11, T12 (weekly); T10 (rare). Frequencies are hypotheses (`design/brief.json`).
* Problem: the baseline ships 19 hex colours, one light theme, three status tones and no status shapes. UNKNOWN is a box tinted `#fff3d9` that differs from its ground by 0.032 dE_OK (MEASURED, 1.6 times a just-noticeable difference), 3 of the 6 non-text pairs (of 22 pairs enumerated) in `app.css` are below 3:1 (secondary-button border 1.68, input border 1.74, state-node border 1.56, strict reading of SC 1.4.11), and its only status pair (green against danger) is 0.068 dE_OK at worst under colour-vision-deficiency (CVD) simulation. The Studio must now show eight statuses (six evidence statuses, RUNNING, BLOCKED) plus AI-proposed provenance, three change kinds, up to eight categories and two themes without relying on colour alone (P6).
* Out of scope: glyph geometry (status-display aspect); type sizes; layout and container budgets; the token build script and the `/assets` allowlist change; the forced-colours implementation; the display order of statuses.

## Quantitative model

* Model and formula:
  1. WCAG 2.2 contrast ratio, evaluated on hex: linear c = c/12.92 if c is at most 0.04045 else ((c + 0.055)/1.055)^2.4; Y = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B; ratio = (Y_light + 0.05)/(Y_dark + 0.05), unrounded.
  2. CVD separation: dE_OK = Euclidean distance in Oklab between two colours after the Machado matrices at severity 50 and 100 for protan, deutan and tritan (plus normal vision), taking the worse of matrices applied to linear and to gamma-encoded sRGB. One just-noticeable difference is 0.02 (CSS Color 4).
  3. Categorical separation: CAM02-UCS Euclidean distance (sRGB viewing conditions: D65 white, Y_b 20, L_A about 4.07 cd/m2) over normal vision and severities 10 to 100 step 10, both conventions.
  4. APCA Lc, advisory only, computed with the W3 reference implementation `apca-w3` 0.1.9 (revision 2022-07-03) on the token hexes, minimum of the absolute value over the four surfaces; it reproduces black on white 106.04, white on black -107.88 and `#262422` on `#F8F6F4` 97.21. An earlier draft used a scratch port that did not reproduce the reference; the values below are the corrected ones.
* Inputs and sources: tokens in `design/tokens/color.tokens.json` (hex); baseline colours from `app.css`; matrices from the authors' page, cross-checked with `colorspacious` 1.1.2 (scratch); glyph renders approximated from the evidence aspect's glyph table; thresholds from the sources above, or marked as our hypothesis. Worked example: `#262422` = (38, 36, 34) has linear (0.01938, 0.01764, 0.01600) and Y = 0.01789; `#F8F6F4` has Y = 0.92400; ratio = (0.92400 + 0.05)/(0.01789 + 0.05) = 14.346. Oracles: black on white 21.000; `#767676` on white 4.542; deuteranopia severity 100 of linear (1, 0, 0) is (0.367322, 0.280085, 0), `#A39000`.

* Decision flips inside the band? No. The chosen palette beats the contrast-ladder palette in both themes under every combination tested (severity 100 only or 50 and 100; linear, gamma or both): chosen worst pair 0.046 to 0.061, ladder about 0.02 to 0.03 (0.021 to 0.030 here; an independent rebuild from the tiers gave 0.022 to 0.028). The comparison favours the chosen palette by construction: it was optimised for this metric and the ladder was not. The ladder hexes are in the design document (7.3) and the token file. An intermediate dark palette did flip (pass against not-run 0.010 under the gamma convention); the shipped tokens were re-searched under both conventions and do not. Contrast ratios are deterministic and have no band. KLM and Fitts are not applicable to colour.
* Validity limits: the CVD matrices model dichromacy and anomalous trichromacy for large fields and were validated "to some degree" (Petroff); 0.02 is a large-patch constant, not a floor for 12 px glyphs; CAM02-UCS fits large uniform patches; the linear or gamma question is unresolved by the sources; APCA is unratified and its size and weight lookup was not read; WCAG ratios ignore polarity, size and weight; nothing here models reading, learning or errors; population prevalence figures are from an author page.
* Deliberate deviations from the model: `status.not-run.fg` is 3.32:1 in light (lowest emphasis, above the 3:1 gate) while other statuses are at least 4.56:1; `text.secondary` is Lc 74.6 in light and 60.7 in dark, below the 75 body minimum, kept because raising it collapses the hierarchy against `text.primary`; category marks keep 3:1 although WCAG does not require it when a label is present; the COL-07 threshold was restated from 0.05 to 0.04 (2 x JND) after the corrected dark palette scored 0.046, and every threshold typed "hypothesis" (COL-07 to COL-12) was chosen at or just below the first accepted value; both are disclosed in section 6 of the design document. The 8 status colours are separated by 6.6 to 7.0 in CAM02-UCS (categories reach 13), so the status floor is lower than the categorical one on that scale; status is redundantly coded. The light UNKNOWN and BLOCKED colours are near-ink and are separated by silhouette alone (0.313, provisional geometry), the weakest spot for the invariant that UNKNOWN is loud; study CS1 covers it.

## Decision

Chosen option: "D, with diff option I and categorical option M", because it is the only option built and measured here that has zero gate failures (option P, existing OSS systems, was not run through the lint) and a worst CVD pair above 2 times the just-noticeable difference in both themes under both simulation conventions (evidence rows for WCAG, Machado, Petroff; model table), while keeping every status separable by glyph and word rather than hue.

Concrete behaviour and values (the token file is authoritative):

1. **Format.** `design/tokens/color.tokens.json`, DTCG 2025.10: `colorSpace` oklch, `components`, `alpha` 1, 6-digit `hex`; `color.light.*` and `color.dark.*` with identical paths; 71 tokens per theme (51 literal, 20 aliases: `focus.ring`, `accent.solid`, `selection.wash`, `status.proposed.*`, `status.running.*`, `canvas.*`, `category.unassigned.*`). Gates run on the hex. sRGB only.
2. **Roles.** Surfaces `canvas`, `raised`, `overlay`, `sunken`; text `primary`, `secondary`, `disabled`; borders `subtle`, `control`; accent (hue 250) with `fg`, `tint`, `on-solid`; eight statuses and RUNNING, each with `fg` and `tint`; diff `added`, `removed`, `changed`, each with `fg`, `tint`, `strong`; categories 1 to 8 (`mark`, `tint`) plus `unassigned`; `canvas.*` aliases for the canvas aspect.
3. **Hues.** Accent 250. Status: pass 165, fail 30, stale 85, conflict 350. UNKNOWN, NOT_RUN, BLOCKED neutral, chroma 0. AI-proposed and RUNNING reuse the accent. Diff: added 207, removed 300, changed 125. Categories 25 + 45k degrees, used in the order 206, 70, 295, 25, 115, 160, 250, 340.
4. **Grammar.** Status hue only on the glyph; status words in `text.primary`; at most one status wash per viewport; UNKNOWN count shown even at zero. Diff on a 3 px rail, a sign (+, -, ~), a word and a row wash; removed text struck through; never on a glyph slot. Category marks always with a name, off by default, at most 8.
5. **Lightness and chroma rules** R1 to R12 (design document section 4), including neutral chroma at most 0.010, chromatic status chroma 0.10 to 0.16, washes at L 0.94 (light) and 0.27 (dark), dark elevation by surface step plus border, no shadow.
6. **Gates.** COL-01 to COL-14 (design document section 6; also in the token file). Computation failure or a missing token is UNKNOWN and fails the build.
7. **Dark theme.** Status glyph colours at Lc 45 or more (NOT_RUN 30), `text.secondary` at Lc 60 or more, on top of the 4.5:1 and 3:1 gates.
8. **Delivery.** `tokens.css` generated with two blocks (`prefers-color-scheme` and a `data-theme` override), hex values, no `light-dark()`; forced-colours mapping to system colours proposed to the accessibility aspect.

Invariants and principles preserved. I3 and P1: UNKNOWN is a neutral, loud glyph with its word and a count that is shown at zero, never tinted like PASS, and PASS is not the loudest mark. I1 and I4: no colour token grants or implies authority; `accent.solid` is a style, and the token file states that no AI or provider surface may render Approve or Apply in any style. I2: the rendered CSS is generated from, and its contrast was computed on, the token file. I5: colour is data delivered by an external stylesheet; no inline style, no provider-supplied colour. P6: every status token carries a word and a glyph id, and hue separation stays above 2 x JND as an added channel, not as the carrier. P9: the AI-proposed treatment uses the accent hue, a dashed outline and the word AI-proposed. Selection uses the same accent hue, so AI-proposed is told apart from selection by form (dashed against solid edge) and word only; this is a disclosed weakness and is measured in CS1 and CS3 (AI-proposed read as owner-selected). P12: every number here is PREDICTION or MEASURED and no benefit is claimed.

## Validation plan

* Prototype measurements (one browser at a time, at 1440x900 and 1280x720; the numbered items are the measurements): (1) token lint for COL-01 to COL-14 in Python from the token file; (2) axe-core `color-contrast` on every view, state and theme (accessibility aspect D11); (3) CVD renders: Machado matrices applied to screenshots of the excursion views, checking that every status word and glyph is present and re-running COL-08 on the real glyph geometry; (4) regeneration test: `tokens.css` equals a fresh generation; (5) hue count per viewport from the slop-budget script; (6) forced-colours smoke test in Chromium; (7) benchmark: run the COL-01 to COL-12 lint on the status and diff tokens of Radix Colors and Primer primitives (option P) and record where they pass or fail, before the palette is frozen. Layout JSON, KLM and latency probes are not applicable to colour.
* Pass criteria, fixed before running (the CVD, glyph, hue-gap, category and elevation floors were set at or just below the current values, so they catch regressions and do not validate the design): 0 gate failures on COL-01 to COL-05, COL-13, COL-14; any UNKNOWN fails; COL-07 at least 0.04, COL-08 at least 0.25, COL-09 at least 35 degrees and 0.02, COL-10 at least 12; axe reports 0 `color-contrast` violations and every `incomplete` result resolved by hand; default viewport shows at most 5 chromatic hues.
* User-study protocol reference: [docs/hci/design/color-system.md](../hci/design/color-system.md) section 12 (CS1 to CS5).
  * Design: within-subject on the excursion evidence rail and change review; CS1 conditions glyph plus word plus hue (proposed), glyph plus word, hue plus word (diagnostic), counterbalanced; CS2 dark text at 4.5:1 against Lc 60 against Lc 75; CS3 diff options G, H, I, J; CS4 categories with and without labels.
  * Participants and sample size: CS1 at least 12 with protan or deutan CVD screened with a standard test and 12 with normal vision (recruitment may cap this, then results are formative); CS2 and CS3 at least 20; formative rounds of about 5 find problems but do not estimate prevalence; a keyboard-only cohort for CS3.
  * Tasks: T02, T03, T06, T08, T14 on the excursion workflow with seeded UNKNOWN, STALE and CONFLICT rows.
  * Measures: time on task; error count; UNKNOWN items misread as PASS (target zero); AI-proposed items read as owner-selected (target zero); wrong-family errors between diff and status (target zero); SUS; per-task SEQ; Raw NASA-TLX; reading errors in CS2.
  * Analysis: effect sizes with confidence intervals, pre-registered hypotheses; SUS compared to 68 only with an interval.
  * Stop criteria: stop and redesign if any participant reads UNKNOWN as PASS, or any AI-proposed item as owner-selected, in the proposed condition; stop diff option I if it yields more wrong-family errors than G; keep the current dark text unless the interval excludes zero difference in errors.
* Status of results: none yet. **No user-benefit claim is made until results exist.**

## Consequences

* Good: a tested vocabulary of colour roles for eight statuses, diff, categories and two themes; every gate is computable from one file, so a lint can fail the build; UNKNOWN is neutral, loud and counted at zero; the baseline's three failing non-text pairs and missing dark theme are addressed; hue is an added channel with a stated floor, not the carrier.
* Bad: 71 tokens per theme and a generation step; hue-only separation is lower than raw Okabe-Ito; lightness carries no attention signal, so attention rests on glyph size, weight and position; the status-neutral packing is tight in dark (0.046; 0.0448 over severities 10 to 100); light UNKNOWN and BLOCKED are near-ink and differ by silhouette only; one accent carries selection and AI-proposed, so their distinction rests on form and word; diff hues are unconventional; dark lifts follow an unratified method; the palette is provisional until CS1.
* Cognitive Dimensions impact: visibility (UNKNOWN neutral, loud, counted at zero); role-expressiveness (hue families map to roles: accent interact, status evidence, diff change kind, category grouping); secondary notation (category colour is optional and never the carrier); provisionality (AI-proposed is accent plus dashed outline plus word); error-proneness (a diff row cannot be mistaken for a status by form; a misread is a study measure); premature commitment (none).
* Security and repository impact: no new runtime dependency. Colour is an external stylesheet under `style-src 'self'`; no `style` attribute and no provider colour (ADR-013). `tokens.css` needs the `/assets` allowlist change owned by the security-boundary aspect. Cross-check tooling (`colorspacious`, MIT) stayed in gitignored `.tmp/` and needs no `docs/oss/REGISTER.md` row; the APCA formula was implemented in scratch code only and no APCA code is added (legal review of implementing the formula is open, SYN C10).
* Licence check: no fonts or icons in this decision. Okabe-Ito hex values are a published palette (S12); I ship none unmodified. OKLCH values and hexes in the file are computed data. Petroff's sets are not used as data.
* Revisit when: the option P benchmark (validation plan item 7) shows an existing OSS system meets COL-01 to COL-12 with fewer tokens; CS1 shows any UNKNOWN misread as PASS or a discrimination rate difference between the hue and no-hue conditions that favours option F; CS3 shows more wrong-family errors for option I than G; the status-display aspect changes glyphs (rerun COL-08); a status is added (PARTIAL needs a kernel ADR); WCAG 3 fixes a contrast algorithm or APCA is ratified; `light-dark()` becomes widely available (drop the two-block CSS); the CVD simulation convention is settled; any cited source changes.
